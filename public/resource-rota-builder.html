Rules simplified for illustration.

What it checks
01
Quick returnsLess than 11 hours between the end of one shift and the start of the next.
02
Long runs of nightsMore than three nights in a row, when sleep debt builds fastest.
03
Days without restMore than five working days before a day off.
04
Backward rotationLates into earlies, or nights into lates. Forward rotation is easier to recover from.
