About · 06:00 handover

Most fatigue is designed into the work. So it can be designed out.

Georgia's PsyWork is an organisational psychology practice for operations that never switch off. We work with the people who build rotas, run sites and answer for the incident log.

GEORGIA — ON SITE, EARLY MORNING
Why this work

I kept meeting capable people who were being blamed for mistakes their shift pattern made almost inevitable.

Early in my career I spent a lot of time on night shifts, in depots and control rooms, listening to people describe the hours between two and five in the morning. The same story came up again and again. Good people, tired in predictable ways, at predictable points in the rota.

The fixes that organisations reached for were usually about the individual: a wellbeing app, a sleep leaflet, a reminder to take care. The causes were almost always in the design of the work. That gap is where this practice sits.
